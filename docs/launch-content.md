# Kinsburg launch content plan

Instagram: @kinsburgtown. Site: kinsburg.com is not registered yet, so every public post says "link in bio" until it is.

This is the working plan for the first two weeks: profile, positioning, three launch reels, a 14-day calendar, captions, auto-replies, Story templates and what to measure.

---

## 0. What the competitor gets right, and where they're open

From their public profile (checked 11 Nov 2026): 19 posts, about 8,971 followers, highlights named "Playstore", "support link" and "Week 1", and a grid made of the same template every time (big "DAY N" text over an isometric daytime city, subtitle "of building a city where every follower is a citizen").

**Why their first reel spread (the mechanism, not the surface)**
- **Self-relevance plus a promise you can act on.** "Every follower becomes a resident" makes the viewer the subject of the video, and following is enough to take part. That's the main driver.
- **What makes people share it:** "put my friends in it too". People send it so they can be neighbours.
- **Proof that it's real:** the city visibly grows in each "Day N" post, and that keeps people coming back.
- **Not something to copy:** first-mover novelty and timing. The second account to do the idea doesn't get that lift for free. Kinsburg has to win on a sharper hook and a better payoff.

**Where they're open (we use these quietly and never mention them in public)**
1. **The payoff is vague.** "Find your character" puts the work on the user. Kinsburg gives you a specific address, job, number and card within a minute of commenting.
2. **Every post looks the same.** It's always daytime and always the same layout, so the grid reads as one post repeated. Kinsburg's night city gives a second look they don't have.
3. **The "Day N" numbering has gaps** (9, 11, 15, 17, 18, 26 are visible). A daily series has to actually go out every day. Ours is generated automatically, so it can.
4. **They counted likes as votes.** Meta demotes "like to vote" posts as engagement bait. Our votes are one per resident, with no like-votes.
5. **Nothing to post.** They have no shareable artefact. Our resident card is designed to be posted to Stories, so every new resident advertises the city.

## 2. Positioning: how Kinsburg is different

Use these as internal talking points. In public, just show these things; never compare.

- **You get a real place, instantly.** Comment MOVE IN and within a minute you have a street address, a job and a resident number, plus a Story-size resident card you can post. You don't have to hunt for yourself, and the reply finds you.
- **The city lives on your clock.** It turns to night when it's night where you are, and every glowing window is a real resident. Opening it at 11 pm looks different from opening it at noon, which gives people a reason to come back and a look nobody else has.
- **Your house grows the longer you stay.** Tenure and taking part (voting, town decisions) add to your house over time. Supporter perks are cosmetic status only: a gold star, a golden roof, your name on the Founders Monument, naming a street. Money never buys a vote, a bigger plot or a better job.

## 3. Launch reels

### Hook shortlist (generated across mechanisms, then scored)
| # | Hook | Mechanism | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every lit window in this city is a real person. Yours is still dark. | Curiosity + identity + gentle loss aversion | **Pick.** It's visual, true, and doesn't need sound |
| 2 | Comment two words. Get a house in this city. | Specific action + reward | **Pick.** It's the clearest how-it-works |
| 3 | The city picks your job. Someone's getting lighthouse keeper. | Curiosity + identity, and drives comments | **Pick.** It brings in a second wave of comments ("I got Baker") |
| 4 | This city gets dark when you do. | Novelty | Strong line, but a weaker call to action. Use it in the series |
| 5 | Resident #1 gets the house by the lighthouse. | Scarcity | Only true once. Save it for a Trial Reel on day 1 |
| 6 | I built a city and I'm giving every follower a house. | Promise | Too close to the competitor's hook. Skip |
| 7 | Your address is 14 Larkspur Lane. (Probably.) | Personal specificity | Good for a Story or Trial Reel test |
| 8 | 0 residents. Watch what happens when you comment. | Open loop | Weaker than #2 |

**Favourite: #1.** The competitor's line *tells* you you'll be a resident. Ours *shows* the city full of real people and points at the one empty spot, so the viewer feels the gap before the call to action arrives.

**Shooting notes for all three**
- Shoot vertical at 1080×1920 (screen-record the city in a portrait window or crop to it). Hide the cursor and browser chrome.
- Keep hook text, faces of cards and the call to action in the centre safe zone: away from the top 220 px and the bottom 400 px.
- No watermark from another app. Add music in Instagram, not in the editor.
- Write each reel so it works muted, with captions burned in.
- **Truth check:** lit windows must be real residents. Seed the city before launch with beta testers, friends and the waitlist. If there are fewer than about 50, film tight on one full street rather than a half-empty wide shot. Never fake windows.

## 4. First 14 days

### The recurring automated series: "Lights on"
Generated from the live city every day at a fixed time (around 20:00 in the creator's timezone):
- **Feed reel or Story clip (8–10 s):** a dusk-to-night time-lapse over the newest street, with windows switching on.
- **Auto overlay** (filled in from city data): `Lights on · night {N}` / `{residents_total} residents` / `+{new_today} moved in today` / `New street: {street_name}` / `Resident of the day: @{handle}, {job}, {address}`.
- **Resident of the day:** picked at random from residents who have opted in to being featured. Never feature someone who hasn't opted in.
- **"New street opened" trigger:** when a street fills up, the next one gets its own Story frame ("Larkspur Lane is full. Quince Row opens tonight.").
- **Cadence:** a Story every night from day 1. In the feed, as a reel, on days 5, 7, 10 and 12, plus any day the numbers jump (new milestone or new street). That keeps the grid varied.

**Supporter announcement rules (day 13), so it isn't engagement bait**
- No "comment SUPPORT", no countdown, no "only 50 spots", no tying it to the election.
- Say plainly: "Everything that matters in Kinsburg (your house, your vote, your job) is free. Supporting adds cosmetic status only."
- The Founders Monument can honestly say "supporters during the founding month" if that's true, with no artificial urgency beyond that.
- Don't run it as a reel. Keep the reel slots for reach, and let the carousel reach the people who already care.

**Election rules (days 8–14)**
- One resident, one vote, on the site by resident number, or one Story poll vote per account. Never count likes, shares or tags as votes; Meta demotes that.
- Nominations come from residents only. Show the real vote count. Post results even if turnout is small.

**Reactive room:** keep days 6 and 12 swappable. If a reel beats the median by 2×, replace the planned post with a variation of the winner (see section 6).

### Auto-replies to MOVE IN comments
**Set-up rules**
- Use an official Instagram API tool (Meta-approved comment-to-DM automation). Send a short public reply plus one private reply (DM) with the card.
- Rotate templates at random and never send the same one twice in a row; identical replies get flagged as spam. Space replies out rather than firing hundreds a second.
- Match the trigger loosely: "move in", "MOVE IN!", "movein", "move in pls" all count. Don't reply automatically to comments that also contain a question or a complaint; send those to a human.
- Variables: `{handle}` `{address}` `{street}` `{job}` `{n}` (resident number) `{card_link}`.

**Public replies (short, varied)**
1. Keys are yours, {handle}. {address}, {job}. Your card's in your DMs.
2. Welcome to Kinsburg. You're resident #{n}, living at {address}.
3. Done. {address} is yours. Your window lights up tonight.
4. {job} at {address}. Good fit. Card's in your DMs.
5. Moved in. Say hi to your neighbours on {street}.
6. Resident #{n} has arrived. You're at {address}.
7. The city made you {job}. No take-backs. Card's in your DMs.
8. Light's on at {address}. Welcome home.
9. {street} just got one neighbour friendlier. Welcome, {handle}.
10. House built, kettle on. {address} is all yours.
11. Welcome in, {handle}. Resident #{n}, {job}, {address}.
12. You live here now. {address}. We'll leave the porch light on.

**Edge cases**
- Already a resident: "You already live at {address}, {handle}. One house each, but it grows the longer you stay."
- Not following (only if moving in is follower-gated): "Your house is ready. Follow @kinsburgtown, then comment MOVE IN again for your keys."
- System busy or queued: "Lots of people moving in at once. Your house is queued and the keys will be in your DMs shortly."
- Comment contains a question: no auto-reply. A human answers.

**Private reply (DM)**
> Welcome to Kinsburg, resident #{n}.
> Address: {address}
> Job: {job}
> Your resident card: {card_link}
> Post it to your Story and tag @kinsburgtown. We reshare new neighbours every night. Your window lights up when it's dark where you are.

### Resident card (the share loop)
- 1080×1920. Shows: Kinsburg wordmark, resident number, handle, address, job, house thumbnail (a night version if it's night for the viewer), and a small footer: "Comment MOVE IN on @kinsburgtown to get yours."
- Keep all text out of the top 250 px and bottom 340 px, where Story UI sits.
- Privacy: a resident can hide their handle on the card and opt out of features from the site or by DM.

### Story sequence for resharing resident cards (nightly, 6–8 frames)
1. **Opener:** a night shot of the newest street. Text: "New neighbours tonight". Sticker: resident counter.
2–5. **Reshares:** up to 4 tagged resident cards. Add a sticker with the street name ("Welcome to Quince Row") on each. Use only cards residents posted themselves and tagged us in.
6. **Their street:** a night view with the new houses circled, labelled with resident numbers (not handles, unless the card already showed them).
7. **Interaction:** a poll or question tied to the street ("What should Quince Row get: bakery or bandstand?"). This feeds the weekly town decision.
8. **Call to action:** "Not a resident yet? Comment MOVE IN on the pinned reel." Add a link sticker to the site once it's live.

Save the best frames to the "Neighbours" highlight each week.

## 6. What to measure each week and how to decide

Pull these numbers from Instagram's native Insights each Sunday, along with city data (residents, cards issued, votes).

### Weekly numbers
| Area | Metric | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Reach | Views per reel, share of views from non-followers | Is the hook reaching new people? |
| Hook | Skip rate / 3-second hold, average watch time | Did the first 1.5 s work? |
| Spread | Sends per reach (aim for 1–2% or more), saves | The strongest signals for reaching new people |
| Join | MOVE IN comments per 1,000 views, new residents | Is the call to action converting? |
| Share loop | Cards shared to Stories ÷ cards issued, tags received | Is the resident card doing its job? |
| Profile | Profile visits → follows rate | Is the bio converting visitors? |
| Retention | Story views ÷ followers, "Lights on" completion rate | Are residents coming back? |
| Town hall | Votes cast ÷ residents | Is the community actually active? |

### Decision rules
- **A format beats the median by 2× on views or sends per reach:** make 3 variations within the next 7 days. Keep the structure, change the hook or the subject.
- **A format comes in under 0.5× the median twice:** retire it, or rewrite only the first 1.5 s and test that as a Trial Reel before putting it back in the feed.
- **Skip rate is worse than the account average:** change the opening frame and hook text only. Don't touch the rest.
- **Lots of views but few MOVE IN comments (under about 5 per 1,000 views):** move the call to action on screen earlier (by about 4 s) and say "MOVE IN" out loud.
- **Lots of MOVE IN comments but few cards shared (under 10% of cards issued):** redesign the card (bigger house image, night version, a fun job line) before spending more on reach.
- **Profile visits rise but the follow rate drops:** fix the bio and pinned posts before making more content.
- **"Lights on" is below the median 3 days running:** keep the series and change the overlay hook (lead with the milestone, the new street or the resident of the day).
- **Turnout under 10% of residents:** make voting easier (a Story poll alone counts) before running the next election.
- **Follower count is not a decision metric.** Residents, sends and cards shared are.

These thresholds are starting points. After two weeks, replace them with the account's own medians.
