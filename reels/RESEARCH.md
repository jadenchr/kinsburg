# What works for "follower city" Reels (research, 18 Nov 2026)

I looked at @meadowvalecity and the accounts that have copied the idea in the last month, read the captions and view counts of their Reels, and checked Instagram's current hashtag rules.

## Who's doing this

| Account | Idea | How it's doing |
|---|---|---|
| @meadowvalecity (9,030 followers, 23 posts) | "Every follower becomes a resident in my city", Day N episodes | Best Reels 10–15K views (Days 4–9); recent ones 2–3.5K |
| @digicitys | "India's own digital city": claim a free plot with your handle | One post: 8,045 likes, **2,260 comments** |
| @wowci.ty (Russian) | "I'm building a city from followers"; followers vote on the city's name | 665 comments on one post |
| @bedcodes | "Every building in Warsaw", built from open data | 3,073 likes |
| @commentville.town | **Comment MOVE IN** and your house appears; comment more to level up | 131 comments. Almost the same pitch as Kinsburg |
| @conquest.city, @whatifborz, @mayor_bob_city, @madeofus.project | Episodes with budgets, votes, jobs and prisons | 23–36 comments each |
| @followtomoveincity | "Bigger account, bigger house" | 5 comments |

## What the winners have in common

1. **A running episode number.** Meadowvale's best posts all start "Day N of every follower becomes a resident in my city". People come back for the next day.
2. **Every post asks for a comment that changes the city.** Meadowvale: "Comment what we should build first: the most-liked idea gets built". wowci.ty: vote on the name. digicitys: "comment your state and city". The posts with the most comments got the most views.
3. **Real names in the story.** conquest.city and whatifborz tag the residents who won a vote or got a job. They share it, and their friends see it.
4. **Stakes and small dramas.** Elections (Meadowvale's "Comment MAYOR"), a mafia (Meadowvale's latest: "pay or refuse?"), budgets, prison votes.
5. **Local pride works.** digicitys (India) and bedcodes (Warsaw) get the biggest numbers by being about a place people belong to.
6. **Hashtags don't drive it.** Meadowvale's five best Reels had **no hashtags at all**. Its later posts used 3–12 and got far fewer views. That's mostly because early posts rode novelty, but hashtags clearly weren't what made them work.

## Hashtag rules now

- **5 hashtags maximum per post or Reel** since 18 December 2025, confirmed by Adam Mosseri; hashtags in a first comment count toward the 5 ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/no-more-hashtag-stuffing-instagram-limits-hashtag-usage-to-just-5-per-reel-and-post-507444-2025-12-19), [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/)).
- Mosseri: hashtags are **for search, not reach**; generic tags like #reels or #explore don't help ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/)).
- Instagram reads the caption, the words on screen and the spoken audio for search, so **say what it is in plain words** in the caption ("a cozy isometric city where every house is a real person") ([Toptal](https://www.toptal.com/creator/post/instagram-seo), [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-reels-guide/)).
- Alt text is another search signal, and it helps screen-reader users ([Toptal](https://www.toptal.com/creator/post/instagram-seo)).
- Instagram no longer shows post counts on hashtag pages, so I couldn't measure tag sizes. What's visible: **#cozygames** is busy with cozy-game recommendations and desk setups; **#isometric** and **#citybuilder** are where the follower cities and city maps sit.

## Hashtags to use (5 per Reel)

Always these 4:

`#kinsburg #citybuilder #isometric #cozygames`

`#kinsburg` is your own tag: every post adds to it, and people searching "kinsburg" find all of them (Meadowvale and Commentville do the same).

Plus 1 that fits the Reel:

| Reel is about… | 5th tag |
|---|---|
| night, lights, the city waking up | `#nightcity` |
| how it works, building updates | `#buildinpublic` |
| the city as a game, features | `#indiegame` |
| close-ups, pretty shots | `#isometricart` |
| how it's made | `#gamedev` |

## Ideas for the next Reels (from what's working)

- **Make it a Day N series.** "Day 3 of building a city where every house is a real person." Keep the number going in every caption.
- **A vote in every post.** Name the first street, pick the next building, choose the first mayor. Kinsburg already has polls (docs/RUNNING.md). The vote is the comment people leave, and it brings them back for the result.
- **"Comment MOVE IN + your country."** Local pride drove digicitys' 2,260 comments, and Kinsburg already follows each viewer's clock. A "lights on in Mumbai, London, New York" Reel fits.
- **Shout out real residents** who said CAST ME: "Meet #2, @itsjadenchristy, the town's first …".
- **Stand apart from @commentville.town**, which has the same MOVE IN pitch. Kinsburg's difference is that the lights follow your clock, you get a job and a resident card, and resident numbers are forever. Lead with those.
