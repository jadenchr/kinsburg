Kinsburg

Privacy

Last updated 12 November 2026

Kinsburg is a live online city where people who follow @kinsburgtown on Instagram can get a house. It is run by Kinsburg Town ("we"). Kinsburg is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.

What we store

  • Your Instagram username, when you comment MOVE IN on one of our posts, send us MOVE IN in a message, or buy a perk. Your username, house, address, job and move-in day are shown publicly in the city, because that is the point of it.
  • The ID of the comment or message that moved you in, so we never move you in twice.
  • Perk purchases: which perk you bought and the username you gave. Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card details or email address. Stripe's own privacy policy applies to the payment: stripe.com/privacy.
  • Votes you make in town elections and decisions, so each resident votes once.
  • How many times you have commented on our posts, which makes your house grow. We don't store what the comments say.

What we don't do

  • We don't use cookies or trackers, and we don't run ads based on you.
  • We don't sell or share your information with anyone.
  • We only read comments and messages sent to @kinsburgtown. We never read your other activity.

Who processes it

The city runs on Cloudflare, which keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the service. Instagram data comes from Meta's official Instagram API. Payments go through Stripe.

Delete your data

Message @kinsburgtown on Instagram with the words MOVE OUT, or contact info.jadenn@gmail.com. Within 30 days we remove your username from the city and its database. Your former house stays as an empty "vacant lot", so nobody else's address changes. Purchase records that tax law requires us to keep are kept for as long as the law requires, without being shown anywhere.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (see above). We answer within 30 days.

  • In the EU, UK and EEA: we use your username to run the city you asked to join (performing what you asked for), and purchase records because the law requires them. You can object, ask for a copy of your data, and complain to your local data protection authority.
  • In the USA, including California: we don't sell or share your personal information, and we don't use it for targeted advertising. You won't be treated differently for using any of these rights.
  • In India: you have the rights given by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including access, correction, erasure and naming someone to act for you. Write to info.jadenn@gmail.com.

Our services (Cloudflare, Stripe and Meta) may process data outside your country. They use the standard legal safeguards for those transfers.

Children

Kinsburg is not meant for children under 13, the minimum age for Instagram.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes are announced on @kinsburgtown.
