Kinsburg

Privacy

Last updated 12 November 2026

Kinsburg is a live online city where people who follow @kinsburgtown on Instagram can get a house. It is run by [owner's full name or business name] ("we"). Kinsburg is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.

What we store

  • Your Instagram username, when you comment MOVE IN on one of our posts, send us MOVE IN in a message, or buy a perk. Your username, house, address, job and move-in day are shown publicly in the city, because that is the point of it.
  • The ID of the comment or message that moved you in, so we never move you in twice.
  • Perk purchases: which perk you bought and the username you gave. Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card details or email address. Stripe's own privacy policy applies to the payment: stripe.com/privacy.
  • Votes you make in town elections and decisions, so each resident votes once.
  • How many times you have commented on our posts, which makes your house grow. We don't store what the comments say.

What we don't do

  • We don't use cookies or trackers, and we don't run ads based on you.
  • We don't sell or share your information with anyone.
  • We only read comments and messages sent to @kinsburgtown. We never read your other activity.

Who processes it

The city runs on Cloudflare, which keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the service. Instagram data comes from Meta's official Instagram API. Payments go through Stripe.

Delete your data

Message @kinsburgtown on Instagram with the words MOVE OUT, or contact [contact email]. Within 30 days we remove your username from the city and its database. Your former house stays as an empty "vacant lot", so nobody else's address changes. Purchase records that tax law requires us to keep are kept for as long as the law requires, without being shown anywhere.

Children

Kinsburg is not meant for children under 13, the minimum age for Instagram.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes are announced on @kinsburgtown.
