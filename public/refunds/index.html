Refunds

Last updated 12 November 2026

Perks normally appear on your house within a few minutes of paying.

You get a full refund if

  • your perk hasn't appeared within 24 hours,
  • you entered the wrong username and we can't move the perk for you,
  • you bought by mistake and ask within 14 days, or
  • we remove your perk under the terms.

How to ask

Message @kinsburgtown on Instagram or email [contact email] with the username you bought for. Refunds go back to the card you paid with through Stripe, usually within 5–10 working days.

Printed posters are made to order, so once a poster has been printed we can only refund it if it arrives damaged or wrong.
